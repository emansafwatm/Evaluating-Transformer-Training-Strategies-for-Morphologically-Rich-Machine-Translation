# Evaluating-Transformer-Training-Strategies-for-Morphologically-Rich-Machine-Translation
> **Repository status:** This repository is a work in progress associated with a manuscript that is currently undergoing substantial revision following an earlier editorial decision. The research design, experiments, results, and conclusions may change during revision. The repository should not yet be treated as a final reproducibility package or cited as a completed study.
>
> ## Development Status

The study is currently being revised. Planned updates include:

- Reassessment of the experimental design and comparison framework.
- Verification and rerunning of model-training experiments.
- Correction and validation of reported metric values.
- Stronger separation between zero-shot and fine-tuned results.
- Consistent documentation of hardware, software, and hyperparameters.
- Statistical reporting across repeated experimental runs.
- Revision of the analysis, conclusions, and generalization claims.
- Release of validated scripts and reproducibility materials when the revised study is stable.
